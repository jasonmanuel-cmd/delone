FAQ

Quick answers.

How fast can a driver be there?

In 28 cities, the median pickup time is 22 minutes. Schedule jobs up to 30 days in advance for free.

What if my item is damaged?

Every job includes damage protection from $500 (Solo) to $10K (Fleet). Claims close within 72 hours.

Do you integrate with Shopify?

Yes — Shopify, WooCommerce, Square, and a public REST API + webhooks for custom builds.

Are drivers vetted?

Background checks, MVR review, vehicle photos, and a 5-job probation rating. Drivers below 4.7 are off the platform.
